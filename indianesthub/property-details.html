About This Property

Presenting a beautiful 3 BHK Independent Builder Floor for sale in the prime location of Zirakpur, Panchkula. This well-designed property offers an excellent blend of modern construction and thoughtful layout, making it perfect for families seeking comfort and convenience.

The property spans 1,250 sq. ft. of carpet area and features spacious bedrooms, modern bathrooms, and a well-ventilated living area with natural light. Located on the 2nd floor with east-facing orientation, the property enjoys excellent air circulation and morning sunlight.

Vastu-compliant construction, premium vitrified flooring, modular kitchen setup, and gated society with 24/7 security make this an ideal investment. Close proximity to Chandigarh, excellent road connectivity via VIP Road, and access to top schools, hospitals, and malls make this a perfect family home.
