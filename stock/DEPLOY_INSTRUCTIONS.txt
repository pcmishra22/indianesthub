WHAT'S IN THIS PATCH
=====================
Two rounds of fixes, all tested (151 backend checks + 14 frontend logic
checks, all passing):

ROUND 1 — EOD Report clarity (Titan bought twice, then sold)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Your multi-occurrence tracking (a stock getting a fresh Buy/Sell row each
time a new signal fires after the last one closed) was already correct —
verified with a standalone script simulating exactly your scenario. The
report SCREEN was the gap: no current-price column, current price only as
fresh as the last Watchlist tick, and repeat rows for one stock could
appear scattered instead of grouped. Rebuilt the "Momentum+AI Report" (EOD
tab) as a trader-focused view:
  - KPI cards up top: Open / Achieved / Not Achieved / Success Rate
  - Filter chips: All/Open/Achieved/Not Achieved, and Both Engines/Momentum/AI
  - Each pick is now a card with a visual progress bar from entry → target
    with a marker for the live current price, colored green/red by whether
    it's moving favorably
  - Repeat picks for one stock show an occurrence badge ("2nd pick", "3rd
    pick") and are sorted together — open positions first (closest to
    target first), then most-recently-achieved, then not-achieved
  - Current price is now always live-refreshed against the actual quote
    when the report loads, not just whatever the last Watchlist tick saw

ROUND 2 — the screenshot you sent (Titan in BOTH Top 5 Buy and Top 5 Sell)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real bug, now fixed. Every tracked stock was being scored twice —
independently as a hypothetical Buy candidate and as a hypothetical Sell
candidate — and the Top 5 Buy / Top 5 Sell leaderboards were each just
"top 5 by that side's score," with nothing stopping the same stock (like
Titan) from scoring decently on both sides and landing in both lists at
once. That's not an actionable signal — you can't buy and sell the same
stock on the same tick. Fixed so each stock is placed on whichever side it
scores strictly higher on; ties break toward whichever extreme rank it
currently holds (top gainer → Buy, top loser → Sell). Added a dedicated
regression test simulating a choppy "Titan-style" stock that scores on
both sides, confirming it now only ever appears on one list.

Also added: a live "as of HH:MM:SS" timestamp on the Top 5 Buy/Sell
leaderboard headers and on the big Buy/Sell headline cards, so you can see
how fresh a given ranking actually is.

Login/session: reviewed users.php + auth.php end to end. Didn't find a
functional bug in sign-in/sign-up — passwords are properly hashed with
password_hash()/password_verify(). Did find one thing worth hardening:
the session ID wasn't being regenerated after a successful login/signup,
which is a mild session-fixation risk (an attacker who got a victim to use
a known session ID before login could inherit their post-login session).
Fixed — session_regenerate_id() now runs right after auth succeeds. If you
were seeing a different, specific login problem (e.g. getting logged out
unexpectedly, or a particular error), tell me what you're actually seeing
and I'll dig into that specifically.
